Questions

Pumping questions

The things people ask before they book. If yours isn't here, call and ask.

How do you know my tank's rated size?

It's usually stamped on the tank lid or listed on the as-built drawing Clark County keeps on file for permitted systems. Tell us what you find when you call.

What if my tank holds more than its rated size?

Some tanks are fuller than rated because of a high water table or a slow drain field. Anything pumped over the rated capacity is the same $0.55 per gallon in Cowlitz County.

How often should I pump?

Every three to five years for most households. The care guide has a calculator that uses your tank size and household size.
