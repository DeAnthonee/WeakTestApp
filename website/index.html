Questions

Straight answers

The things people ask before they book. If yours isn't here, call and ask.

Does Northbank do the work?

No. Northbank takes the booking and passes it to a licensed local partner, who does the pumping or inspection and bills you directly. Northbank does not hold a septic licence.

How is the price worked out?

In Cowlitz County: $0.55 per gallon on your tank's rated size, with disposal included. A 1,000 gallon tank is $550, a 1,500 gallon tank is $825. Tanks up to 750 gallons are the $375 minimum. If more than the rated capacity is pumped, the extra is the same $0.55 per gallon. Sales tax is added.

How often should a septic tank be pumped?

Every three to five years for most households. A 1,000 gallon tank serving four people is due about every two and a half years; a big tank with two people can go longer. The care guide has a calculator.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. The partner needs access to the tank lids and a way to reach you by phone if something comes up. Leave gates unlocked and dogs inside.
