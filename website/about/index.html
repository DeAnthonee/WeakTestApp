Who we are

A booking service for septic work

Northbank Septic books septic pumping, inspections and grease trap service in Cowlitz County and grease trap service in Clark County. Clark County pumping and inspections are on a waitlist. Northbank takes the booking and passes it to a licensed local partner, who does the work and bills you directly.

Northbank does not hold a septic licence and does not do the work itself. Every job is done by a licensed local partner, and Northbank is the one number to call to book it.

Northbank Septic LLC is based in Vancouver, WA. Booking is open in Cowlitz County; Clark County pumping and inspections: join the waitlist.

How pricing works

How the price is set

You should be able to work out your own bill from this website before the visit.

  • The tank's rated size sets the price. In Cowlitz County, $0.55 per gallon, on the size stamped on the lid or on the county drawing, not on how full it looked.
  • Disposal is included. Sales tax is added.
