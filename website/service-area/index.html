How far we go

One price across the route

The truck starts in Vancouver every morning. Where it goes from there depends on the day's route, not on how much we charge you.

  • No trip charge. $0.55 per gallon in Kelso is $0.55 per gallon in Camas.
  • Clark County inspections. Reports of System Status for home sales anywhere in Clark County, filed with Public Health.
  • Cowlitz County on request. Pumping across Woodland, Kalama, Longview and Kelso, inspections under Cowlitz County's process.
  • Grease trap route. Restaurants from Vancouver to Longview on 30, 60 or 90 day plans.
Questions

Service area questions

The short version of where and when.

Do you go outside the cities listed?

Usually. Unincorporated Clark and Cowlitz County are most of our work. Call with the address and we'll tell you on the spot.

Is there a trip charge for the far end of the route?

No. The per-gallon price is the same everywhere we go.

How do you schedule the route?

By area. If a neighbor is being pumped this week, you can usually get the same day. Emergencies jump the line regardless.
