About King Septic Services

A locally owned and operated septic company serving Anytown County and surrounding areas.

We handle everything a septic system needs over its life: routine pumping, inspections for home sales, repairs when something fails, and full system design and installation when it's time for a new one.

Septic work is not glamorous, but it matters. A neglected system is expensive to replace and unpleasant to live with. Our job is to keep yours out of sight and out of mind, and to be straight with you about what it needs and what it doesn't.
