Grease trap questions

What owners and kitchen managers ask before they book.

How often does my grease trap need to be pumped?

Often enough that grease and solids never reach a quarter of the trap's depth. For a small under-sink trap in a busy kitchen that can be every 30 days. For a 1,000 gallon interceptor at a cafe, 90 days may be fine. Ask when you book.
